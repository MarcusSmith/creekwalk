Creek Walk Strikes Back

Last year was so much fun we wanted to do it again, this year in the opposite direction! We learned some lessons from last year: getting better shoes for dad so that he hopefully doesn’t lose any toenails this year and getting an even better lightweight backpack

Mama made croissant sandwiches (and also insisted we take wipes) we also have Dylan’s favorite seaweed snack and turtle chips!

The backpack is light enough (and Dylan is strong enough) that he can carry it!!

Ready to go!!!

Nature preserve

The front entrance is under construction, so we parked in the community garden - where the parking lot is flooded from all the recent rain

The path down is steep and muddy, our first team work balance exercise for the day

The water is higher and colder than normal, so high or starting island from last year is gone

Also, found this cool moth in the parking lot…

Roswell road bridge

We see the Bridge for Roswell road immediately, this might be the biggest road we’ve gone under!

Up close to the bridge

We tried going through the middle but it was way too deep, we decided to hug the edge and fight through trees and rocks instead

Middle of the bridge has lots of smooth rocks, we had to take turns moving so one of us is always stable

Got to the other side, and took a break on an island to clean sand and rocks out of our sandals

The Creek

This end of the creek is tough than the other side, a lot of deep sand and hidden slippery rocks, we keep holding hands the whole way. Found plants in little rapids and tadpoles

We ended up in a sunny clearing, with a rock island where we did another sandal cleaning. We ran into a natural dam of trees leading into the back of a neighborhood

Dylan did scouting to try to find the best way around

Neighborhood

We found the easiest path over the trees to shallow sand and climbed into the back of a neighborhood

The whole creek behind the neighborhood was full of slippery rocks, making it really slow going and took a lot of teamwork. Even then Dada slipped once and got super wet!

Rapids!!

As there were more and more rocks, eventually it turned to rapids!

The water got shallower and the rocks got more slippery, it was no longer possible to stay balanced just on our feet. We started climbing over the rocks in all fours

It was hard work, but the pay off was worth it, we made it to rocks on the side of the rapids and had lunch looking at the rushing water

Calling it

Dylan is growing up fast (turns 6 this month!) and is already more mature than his dad. After having a snack by the rapids he decides that it’s time to turn around, we don’t want to get too tired on the way back. Dad thinks maybe we should look on the map to see how close we are to the park before deciding when to turn around. “Dada, we should turn around no matter where we are on the map.” Good call! (Checked the map anyway)

The nature preserve starting point on the right. The original goal was sneaking through the golf course in Chastain park. We scoped it out earlier in the summer and the creek ran right through the middle but was way lower than the golf course, we didn’t think anyone would see us

Dylan led the way on the way back through the rapids, so that dad could play downstream defense. He mastered his “frog walk” moving through the rocks. “I’m not a noob anymore” (he’s been watching a lot of videos of people playing Minecraft lately)

Just like last year we were able to move a lot faster on the way back, but dad had some slips on rocks cutting his hand. The backpack was the real MVP this trip, even with multiple quick dunks into the river it kept everything dry!

Dad’s phone overheated, but it was best to focus through the rocky creek anyway

The way back

It was nice to eventually hit sandy patches again, so we could walk easier and enjoy the scenery.

In a comedy of errors, dad’s sunglasses fell off his head and onto Dylan’s. When trying to console Dylan, his phone fell into the creek too. The good news is that the phone was pulled out immediately and ok, and Dylan couldn’t stop laughing. We decided maybe we both needed a little break, so we stopped to eat our sandwiches and clean the sand out of our sandals

On the way back we noticed we passed the back greenhouses of our local plant nursery

After moving through rapids for so long, the trek back under the Roswell road was a breeze. We wanted to a friendly man who has set up a chair under the bridge, and we’re back in the nature preserve. The hard sand of the creek in the nature preserve was so easy to walk through. We waved to another person who was out playing fetch with her dog in the creek. The path we took back was crazy muddy so we looked for another way back

Found our entrance from last time (wasn’t quite where we remembered it, but it was still there!)

Made it back to the community garden

Adventure complete!

Learnings

Things we learned this year:

  • Closed toed sandals trap sand way worse than flip flops or boots. But last year Dylan’s boots got too heavy and full of water. Dad would have no toes left if he wore flip flops through all the rocks and rapids. These were definitely the right call.
  • the light water proof backpack is awesome!
  • next year we are going in the other direction, slippery rocky creek is a really challenging walk - we’re exhausted
  • We called it around 2 hours last year, and 2.5 hours in this year, that’s about the right length we would have been miserable if we pushed through even 30 extra minutes
