Dylan Strikes Back

This year, we’re going to try the same goal as our first year, but in reverse. We’re going to start at Windsor Meadows Park and walk down towards our nature preserve

There is a very large road not far from our starting point that we don’t know if we can cross. We’re going to scout it out and see. If we can’t we’ll turn around and go past our starting point in the other direction and scout that side out for next year

Starting out

We packed similar to last year, but with bigger water bottles and more first aid equipment

We set out in the morning this time to get going before it’s too hot. We promised Mom to be safe and have fun. In that order!!

The Park

Nice morning in the park!

And down into the creek

The Creek

Our first obstacle is a big pipe

The left side looks more shallow, but there is some gross looking orange water over near it

No thanks.

We went to the other side where it was deeper. Dad goes first and gets about thigh deep and Dylan jumps into his arms to get carried to shallow water. But we end up in some fine sand that sinks into our sandals and have to stop to clean them out

Morning sun looking nice this time

About halfway to the highway bridge we hit a neighborhood

Hit a patch of deeper water. Dad scouts it out first to find the shallowest path with solid sand. Not deeper than Dylan, but cold in the morning!

The pay off is worth it! Nice break point with a log seat

Had a stretch of “needs both hands” where we had to work together to move through slippery rocks and climb up onto a log to get a resting place. Our larger water bottles fell out of the bag and we had to fish them out of the water.

Stopped to skip rocks

Back to shallow sandy easy moving!

Looks like a dog was playing here earlier, probably from one of the neighborhood houses

Calling It

Hit a patch of creek that seemed to get deep and was very shady (so we couldn’t see the bottom) and decided that was a good place to call it

Dylan “planted” a tree branch to mark our spot

Did a little celebratory rock throwing

The Way Back

Found some fish!

Dad held up a branch to make it easier for Dylan to get by, while Dylan found a flower

Once we made it back to our log break island we stopped and had some snacks

(But not before we cleaned our hands with Mama’s wipes)

Then we made it back to our first obstacle, the pipe. From this side we could we a block under the pipe that made for an easy step, not an obstacle this time!

And then we were back to the park!

Next

We went to scope out where we would go next time, the other direction down the creek from the park

There is a red pedestrian bridge right before a bridge for the next road we would need to pass under.

On our way towards it we found a large butterfly and some cool ferns

Looks like this next bridge is the easiest one yet, just a few inches deep with no slippery rocks

Pretty easy going after getting through it too

A dad and his son who had been throwing a baseball in the park saw us in the creek and went into the walking bridge as we walked under it. The dad had a lot of questions for us, seems like his son (couple years older than Dylan) had wanted to do something similar to us, but he wasn’t sure about it. We told him about our last three adventures and what obstacles we ran into. He seemed pretty excited, we might have inspired some new creek adventurers!

We checked out the top of the walking bridge on our way back, texted Mama to let her know we were safe and then headed home!
