Keeping Momentum

We had a busy summer and missed our normal August time for a creek walk, but we didn’t want to miss a year and lose momentum, so we’re going to do things a little different this year. We’re heading out in mid-September, it’s still kind of swimming temperature, but the water is starting to get cold. We’re setting out early afternoon instead of morning since we’re more concerned with it being too cold rather than too hot.

We are starting at Windsor Meadows Park again, but this time heading north, exploring new territory

We’re also hoping to keep it a bit shorter, and because of that we might have talked Mom into going with us, even if only to the park to wait for us!

Mama loaded us up with snacks and supplies and we were ready to go
