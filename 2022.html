Dylan’s first Creek Walk Adventure

On the last day of summer before kindergarten starts, Dylan and Dada are planning to do a big creek walk from the nature preserve nearby to a park about 2 miles away. We aren’t sure how far we can get, and it looks like the creek crosses several roads

We packed some water and chips and we were on our way!

Nature Preserve

We started our adventure at the Blue Heron Nature Preserve at 2pm. We snapped a picture with the mural wall before making our way down to the Creek

The Creek

We walked into the cold creek and immediately got to skipping rocks and collecting sticks and shells

We made friends with a lady sitting on an island reading a book and found a cone that we poked with our stick

The First Bridge

We hit our first road, with a nice high bridge! 1 down 2 to go!

Neighborhood

After leaving the nature preserve we found ourselves in the back of a neighborhood with some big nice houses

Made another friend

Creek Adventures

We found an actual blue heron!

Had to hold hands on slippery rocks and waded through the highest water we’ve seen

Climbed through some rapids

Caught up with the blue heron again

Second Bridge

The second bridge has a lot of clearance too! But it was protected my slippery rapids and deep water. By holding hands we were able to work through it as a team. Dada starts having flip flop regrets, Dylan starts thinking Dada‘s floatie idea might have been a good one after all…

More Adventures

Bamboo forest!

Big island

Butterflies having lunch

Calling It

At the hour and a half mark we found the heron again. We checked the map and we weren’t even halfway there…..Dylan decided this is a good stopping point, we don’t want to run out of energy on the way back. We stop for a snack and head back

Yikes!

The Way Back Home

Our experience made the way back easier - we had mastered our teamwork balance techniques and breezed through rapids and slippery rock sections. We found an easier path under the second bridge than was much shallower. We had avoided it on the way there because it looked full of trash, but it turned out to be leaves and flower petals. Most of the hardest parts were towards the end, so the walk back got easier and easier and was a nice relaxing finish
